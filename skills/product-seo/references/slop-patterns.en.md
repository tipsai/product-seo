# Machine-sounding English: what to remove

Load this file when the page is in English. Remove these from drafts, and quote them when reviewing.

## Words

delve, pivotal, testament, seamless, robust, leverage, game-changer, unlock, elevate, landscape, standout, powerhouse, comprehensive, moreover, furthermore, additionally.

## Phrases and constructions

- "In today's fast-paced world", "In a world of", "In conclusion".
- "not just X, but Y".
- "whether you're X or Y".
- "serves as", "acts as" where "is" would do.
- Lists of three made for rhythm when there are really two things, or four.
- A long dash used as a dramatic pause. Use a full stop or a comma.
- Rhetorical questions used as transitions.
- Linking sentences that restate what the table next to them already shows.
- More than one list in the "bold lead, then text" shape per page.
- Every item described in the same template with the same length.
- Chains of adjectives.
- Metaphors and decoration.
- Unnamed "experts" and "studies".
- More than one exclamation mark per page.
- Chatbot endings: "I hope this helps".
- Openings of the form "X is a powerful tool that".

## What to keep

- Direct statements: "has", "does", "costs".
- Specifics: names, prices, limits, platforms.
- Sentences of different lengths.
- A detail only someone who opened the product would know.

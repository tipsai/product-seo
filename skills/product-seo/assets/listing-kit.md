## Email to the author of a roundup

```
Subject: <product> for your list of <topic>

Hi <name>,

I read your list of <topic> at <url>. <One specific thing about it.>

I work on <product>. It <what it does, one sentence>. It may fit the list for readers who <who it is for>. The thing it does that the tools on the list do not: <one fact>.

Link: <url>

If it is not a fit, no problem.

<name>
```

## Email to correct a fact

```
Subject: A correction for <article title>

Hi <name>,

Your article at <url> says <what it says> about <product>. The current fact is <fact>, see <official url>.

Thanks for including us.

<name>
```

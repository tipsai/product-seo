# Brief: an ordinary reader looks at the page

Fill the brackets. Attach screenshots of the whole page at desktop width (1440) and phone width (390), in scroll order.

```
You are an ordinary person, not a marketer and not an editor. You typed "[query]" into a search engine because [the situation behind the query]. You clicked a result and landed on the page in the attached screenshots.

First look at the desktop screenshots from top to bottom, the way a person scrolls: fast, reading headings, tables and pictures, stopping only where something answers your question. Then do the same with the phone screenshots.

Answer in plain words:
1. Did you find what you came for? Where on the page? How far did you have to scroll?
2. Where would you have closed the page, and why?
3. What did you skip without reading?
4. Was anything hard to read or to make out, on the desktop or on the phone? Name the place.
5. Did you notice the offer to try the product? Do you understand what you would get? Would you try it? Why or why not?
6. One thing that would have made this page more useful to you.

Do not comment on SEO, style rules or grammar. Do not be polite. If the page lost you in the first screen, say so.
```
